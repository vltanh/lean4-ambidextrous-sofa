# Layout

[Back to the README](../README.md)

The library [`AmbidextrousSofa/`](../AmbidextrousSofa) has 466 modules: the plane, motions, caps and areas in
`Geometry/`, `Measure/`, `Topology/` and `Algebra/`; Romik's ambidextrous sofa in `Reference/`; the bound on
the score of a cap and its equality cases, the bulk of the proof, in `Analysis/`; the main theorems in
[`Theorems/Optimality.lean`](../AmbidextrousSofa/Theorems/Optimality.lean), and in the vocabulary of the Challenge
in [`Main.lean`](../AmbidextrousSofa/Main.lean), with the definitions that the Challenge copies in
[`ChallengeDefs.lean`](../AmbidextrousSofa/ChallengeDefs.lean). [`Analysis/SurfaceAreaFacts.lean`](../AmbidextrousSofa/Analysis/SurfaceAreaFacts.lean)
proves three facts about the surface area measure of a convex body that the copied `MovingSofaOptimality`
proves privately.

[`MovingSofaOptimality/`](../MovingSofaOptimality/README.md) has the 51 modules of the formalization of Baek's
theorem, copied from lean4-moving-sofa; lean4-moving-sofa describes them.

The labels in the docstrings (MS.3, AF1, ED.1, …) are those of the research notes in which the proof was found
([archive](archive/README.md)).
